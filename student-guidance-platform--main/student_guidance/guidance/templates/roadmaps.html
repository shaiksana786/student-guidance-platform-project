Tips for Success in {{ roadmap.name }}

Semester-wise Action Plan:

  • During Semesters 1-2: Master fundamentals, build strong foundation, participate in college clubs
  • During Semesters 3-4: Choose specialization area, take on larger projects, apply for internships
  • During Semesters 5-6: Do relevant internships, contribute to open-source, enhance skills
  • During Semesters 7-8: Focus on placements, prepare interview rounds, mentor juniors

Beyond Classroom:

  • Participate in departmental clubs and projects
  • Attend workshops and seminars by industry experts
  • Contribute to open-source projects relevant to your branch
  • Build a portfolio of projects aligned with your branch
  • Network with seniors and industry professionals
  • Take online certifications to enhance your profile

Preparation for Placements:

  • Start preparation from 3rd year onwards
  • Practice interview questions relevant to your branch
  • Build projects that demonstrate your branch expertise
  • Get strong recommendation letters from professors
  • Maintain good CGPA (above 7.0)
  • Update resume regularly with achievements
