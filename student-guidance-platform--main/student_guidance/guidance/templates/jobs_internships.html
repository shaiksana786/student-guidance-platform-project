      Resume Tips:

      • Length: Keep it to 1 page (for internships) or 1-2 pages (for jobs)
      • Format: Clear hierarchy, easy to read, professional fonts
      • Content: Quantify your achievements - "Improved performance by 25%"
      • Keywords: Use keywords from job description
      • Spacing: Don't overcrowd, leave proper margins
      • ATS Optimization: Use standard resume templates for ATS compatibility
      • Proofreading: Zero typos or grammatical errors

      Resume Template Structure:

      [YOUR NAME]

      Email | Phone | LinkedIn | GitHub

      TECHNICAL SKILLS - Languages, Frameworks, Tools, Databases

      EDUCATION - College, Degree, CGPA, Graduation Year

      PROJECTS - 2-3 relevant projects with descriptions

      INTERNSHIP EXPERIENCE - Company, Role, Duration, Achievements

      CERTIFICATIONS - Online courses, workshops completed

      ACHIEVEMENTS - Hackathons, competitions, awards

      Interview Preparation

      Types of Interviews:

      • Coding/DSA Round: Problem solving, data structures, algorithms
      • Technical Round: System design, project discussions, technical depth
      • HR Round: Behavioral, communication, fit for company culture

      Interview Tips:

      • Practice coding problems on LeetCode and HackerRank
      • Prepare 2-minute introduction about yourself
      • Research the company and their tech stack thoroughly
      • Practice with mock interviews
      • Be ready to explain every project on your resume
      • Ask good questions about the role and company
      • Follow up with thank you email after interview

      🎯 Complete Job Search Strategy

      A structured approach to land your dream job:

      Phase 1: Foundation (1st Year)

      • Master fundamentals: DSA, Core programming languages
      • Build 2-3 small projects and host on GitHub
      • Participate in coding contests (HackerRank, CodeChef)
      • Develop your GitHub profile with clean code
      • Start contributing to open-source projects

      Phase 2: Specialization (2nd Year)

      • Choose a specialization (Web Dev, ML, DevOps, etc.)
      • Apply for summer internships (even unpaid for learning)
      • Build 2-3 medium-scale projects in your domain
      • Get internship letters and learn practical skills
      • Network with professionals in your field

      Phase 3: Advanced Development (3rd Year)

      • Pursue paid internships in target companies
      • Build production-ready projects
      • Practice system design and architecture
      • Start preparing for technical interviews
      • Build strong LinkedIn network and presence

      Phase 4: Job Search (3.5-4th Year)

      • Intensive DSA and system design practice
      • Apply to 10+ companies per week
      • Use referrals and networking effectively
      • Clear coding interviews and HR rounds
      • Negotiate offer and finalize joining
